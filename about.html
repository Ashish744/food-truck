Six years, six trucks, one obsession with getting street food right.

Stackly began as a single truck and a stubborn idea: that food cooked fast, on the street, could still be genuinely great. Here's the story so far.

Brand story

It started with a borrowed truck and a folding table.

In 2019, two line cooks pooled savings for a secondhand truck and parked it outside a college gate. No investors, no business plan beyond "cook it fresh, charge fairly, don't cut corners."

Our journey

Word of mouth did what no ad budget could.

By year two we'd added a second truck. By year four, four more. Every new route was chosen because customers asked us to show up there — not because a spreadsheet told us to.

Food philosophy

If it wouldn't taste good at our own table, it doesn't go on the menu.

We test every new dish on our own crew first. If it doesn't get finished, it doesn't launch — no matter how good it photographs.

Customer experience

We still remember regulars' usual orders.

Six trucks in, the goal hasn't changed: know your customer, cook their food right, and get them back on their way with something worth talking about.

Mission & Vision

What we're building toward.

Two ideas that shape every decision, from menu changes to new routes.

Our mission

Make genuinely good food accessible from a truck window in under ten minutes, without compromising on ingredients, technique, or care.

Our vision

A city where the best meal of your day is just as likely to come from a truck as a restaurant — and where every truck in our fleet earns that trust daily.
